# Patch Notes: World of Daggerfall

## New: World of Daggerfall (Kamer's mod, ported 1:1)
- The wilderness is no longer empty. Bandit camps, rock fields, mountains and other sites now stand between towns across the Iliac Bay, exactly where the mod places them.
- Camps can spring an ambush when you get close: bandits, bears, warriors and treasure. A camp already next to you when it loads in stays quiet.
- Privateer's Hold has its bandit camp outside, with the fires lit.
- Online, everyone in a room sees the same sites in the same places.
- Online, camps are shared: the first player to set one off owns it, and everyone nearby sees and fights the same enemies. Its treasure goes to whoever set the camp off.

## Terrain now matches Daggerfall Unity
- Hills and mountains were about 20% too tall. The whole world now uses Daggerfall Unity's real terrain height.
- Old saves still work. Your character, dropped items, torches, camps, enemies, Recall marks and ship position are moved to the new ground when you load.

## Fixes
- Items and torches dropped inside a house no longer come back in the wrong place after you walk across the map.
- Torches and camps left in a location are now kept in your save.
- If part of the world fails to load, it no longer leaves invisible walls behind or keeps using memory.
- If a region's data fails to download, the game keeps trying in the background and fills it in when it arrives.
- Camps near where you arrive, load in, or step out of a dungeon now behave like Daggerfall Unity's.

## Online note
- If you have the game open from before this update, refresh the page. Until you do, other players may look like they're floating or sunk into slopes.
- A wagon someone parked before the update may sit at the wrong height until its owner logs back in (it clears within 72 hours).
