# Patch Notes: Looted bodies stay looted

## Dungeons (online)
- Bodies that were already looted no longer fill back up with fresh gold and items when you come back into a dungeon. It happened when your level was different from the level of whoever first explored that dungeon, or after you levelled up, because some monsters change type on the way in to match the dungeon everyone shares.
- Bodies nobody has opened still hold their own loot, as before.
- A damaged entry in a dungeon's shared record no longer moves the dead monsters after it onto the wrong spots.
