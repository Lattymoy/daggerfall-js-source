Shadow cache copy check

Enhanced Lighting keeps lamp shadows in a cache and copies them onto the screen's shadow maps every frame a lamp has something moving near it. The old copy (a depth blit) read back whatever the graphics driver made of it on Direct3D, which is what flickered. This page copies a known pattern both ways, the old blit and the new copy, on this machine's GPU, and counts the texels that came back wrong. Nothing on this page flickers: it draws offscreen and prints numbers.
