# Patch Notes: Rise now, and a rest that counts

## Death (online)
- The death screen no longer gets stuck. A message that popped up while you were dead (like a journey's "You have arrived") could hide behind it, and then "Rise now" and the countdown both stopped working. Messages now wait until you have risen.
- Dying ends a Travel Options journey. You are no longer walked on toward your old destination after you rise; the destination is kept, so you can resume it from the travel map.

## Magic (online)
- Resting online wears spell effects down, as it always has offline: each hour you rest takes an hour off your active effects. Casting a buff, resting your magicka back and casting again no longer stacks it forever.

## Survival (online)
- With Survival on, a night's rest outdoors or in a room now pays down your sleep debt and uses food and water, as it does offline and in dungeons. Before, only a dungeon rest did.
