# Patch Notes: Sigil Stones

## Sigil Stones
- Sigil Stones stack. Stones you already have join into one stack the next time you load.
- Sigil Stones are bound: they can't be traded, dropped, sold, or put in a chest or a body. They go in your pack, your wagon or your own storage.

## The Sigil Broker
- Prices are doubled: a set piece costs 4 stones (6 for a Legendary), a set weapon 6, a piece of Ruhn's Regalia 12.
- The Broker counts every stone in a stack. Locked stones are still never spent.
- Everything the Broker sells is bound: it can't be traded, dropped or sold, but can still be repaired and identified.
- Dismantle anything the Broker sold you for half its price back in stones: 2 for a Rare piece, 3 for a Legendary or a weapon, 6 for Ruhn's Regalia. Enhanced Plus: press Dismantle on the item's card. Classic and GrimoireUI: use Remove on the item with no chest or wagon open, then answer Yes. Take it off and unlock it first.
- Items bought before this update are not bound and can't be dismantled.

## Shops
- Items on a shop counter are no longer lost if the window closes on its own (dying, leaving, loading, the quick dial key). They go back to your pack.
- The backup shop menu no longer buys bound or locked items.

## The Burning Court
- The way home where the Warden falls no longer takes you out when you walk into it. Press it to leave, so you can pick up your spoils first.
- Both ways out of the court can now be pressed from up close.
- Leaving the court while offline now puts any spoils left on the floor into your pack.

## Also
- Classic and GrimoireUI: long messages wrap instead of running off the screen, and a right-click no longer answers the Dismantle question.
- Enhanced Plus: the Dismantle question takes Y or N/Enter, clicking outside it or the Info box closes it, and shops no longer show a price for items they won't buy.

No one is disconnected by this update.
