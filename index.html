A 1:1 port of The Elder Scrolls II: Daggerfall

Daggerfall, in your browser.

The 1996 game with its rules intact: the formulas, the quests, the calendar, the crimes, translated line by line from Daggerfall Unity and drawn on a renderer built from scratch. Desktop or phone. Nothing to install.

Before the door opens

Bring your own ARENA2 folder.

This site carries no game data. Daggerfall is free, but its files aren't ours to hand out, so the first thing the game asks for is the folder. Pick it once; it stays in your browser and never leaves your device.

What it is

Ported 1:1

The rules are Daggerfall's. The file formats, the world assembly, the combat and skill math, quests, items, magic, guilds, the calendar and the courts, each translated from Daggerfall Unity's C# with its constants kept byte-exact, quirks and all.

Rebuilt

The presentation is new: a hand-rolled WebGL2 renderer with no engine underneath it, a touch layer so it plays on a phone, and two interfaces. The classic screens, laid out from the original art to the pixel, or an enhanced one built as type and layout instead of painted bitmaps. Same game, one toggle.

In progress

Daggerfall Unity is a decade of work and this is a port of it, arc by arc. What has landed and what hasn't is written down. Read the ledger before you expect a finished game.

How to play

  1. Get Daggerfall.

    It has been free since 2009. Any of these gives you the files; the zip is the same patched set Daggerfall Unity uses.

    • Steam
    • GOG
    • DaggerfallGameFiles.zip

  2. Find the ARENA2 folder.

    It's inside the game folder. From Steam, look under DF/DAGGER/ARENA2. In DaggerfallGameFiles.zip it's arena2/, and on a phone you can hand the game the whole zip instead.

  3. Press Play and pick it.

    On a desktop, choose the folder or drop it on the page. On a phone, choose the zip. The files are read into your browser's storage once and never uploaded; the next visit skips this step. Saves stay in your browser too, so clearing the site's data clears them.

  4. Then play.

    W, A, S and D to move, the mouse to look, Shift to run. Click the game to take the pointer; Escape gives it back and opens the pause menu. On a phone a stick, a look pad and the buttons appear on their own.
