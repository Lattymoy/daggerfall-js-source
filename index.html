DaggerfallOnline

An open-source rebuild of The Elder Scrolls II: Daggerfall that runs in a browser. Same rules, new renderer, nothing to install.

You need your own copy of Daggerfall, free since 2009. Your game files stay on your device. An account is optional - you only need one to play online or back up your saves.

What it is

Reimplemented 1:1

Translated line by line from Daggerfall Unity's C#. Bugs included, on purpose.

Rebuilt

A hand-rolled WebGL2 renderer with no engine underneath, and a procedural sky.

In progress

Not finished. You cannot import classic or Daggerfall Unity saves yet, and there is no mod support. The ledger tracks the rest.

How to play

Three steps, and the first two only happen once.

  1. Get Daggerfall.

    Free from any of these.

    • Steam
    • GOG
    • DaggerfallGameFiles.zip

  2. Press Play and pick your ARENA2 folder.

    On Steam it is under DF/DAGGER/ARENA2. Pick it or drop it on the page; on a phone, hand over the zip. You will not be asked again.

  3. Then play.

    You wake in Privateer's Hold, as everyone does. Touch controls appear on their own.

Controls
Daggerfall Unity's defaults. WASD and mouse, F6 for the pack, F9 and F11 to quick save and load, Esc for the rest. Rebind anything in Settings.
Browsers
Any current one with WebGL2: Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
Saves
In your browser's storage. The desktop app keeps them as real files instead.
The desktop app
Optional. Saves become real files you can back up, it finds your Daggerfall install and reads ARENA2 straight from disk, and it works offline. The Windows installer and the Linux AppImage update themselves before you play; the portable exe and the Mac tell you when a new version is out. WindowsmacOSLinux On Windows the -setup file installs where you choose; the -portable one runs from wherever you put it (get it here). The app is not code-signed yet, so the first launch warns: on Windows choose More info, then Run anyway; on a Mac open it once, then choose Open Anyway under System Settings, Privacy & Security. The Mac build is for Apple silicon. On Linux, make the AppImage executable. Every file, or build it from source.
If it breaks
Reload first. If it is still broken, file it on the issue tracker and say what you did.

What's in it

All of this is in the game now.

The world
The whole Iliac Bay, streamed as you walk it. Weather and seasons run on the game's calendar.
Towns and people
Shops, taverns, banks and guild halls. People who talk and take offence, a watch that arrests you, a court that tries you.
Dungeons
The classic dungeons with their locks, teleporters and traps, and an automap that fills in as you go.
Combat
Daggerfall's own hit and damage formulas. Armour wears out, and enemies run when they are losing.
Magic
The spellbook, the spell and item makers, and the Daedric artifacts.
Your character
The class questions, custom classes and the biography. Then levelling and reputation.
Quests
265 of them, running on the game's own scripts and tracked in the journal.
The screens
Every classic window laid out from the original art, with a modern set beside it. One press switches.
Sound and music
The game's sounds, and its MIDI through a synth written for this project. Your own tracks work too.
