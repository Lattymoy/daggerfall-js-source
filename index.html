What it is

Ported 1:1

The rules come straight from Daggerfall: the file formats, the world, combat math, quests, magic, guilds, even the calendar and the courts. All of it was translated line by line from Daggerfall Unity's C#, and the quirks were kept on purpose.

Rebuilt

The presentation is new. The renderer is hand-rolled WebGL2 with no engine underneath, the sky is procedural, phones get touch controls, and you can use the classic screens or an enhanced pixel-art interface.

In progress

Daggerfall Unity took a decade to build, and this port is catching up piece by piece. Read the ledger before you expect a finished game.

What's in it

Everything here is in the game right now. The ledger keeps the finer print.

The world
The whole Iliac Bay, streamed as you walk it: towns, wilderness, weather and seasons on the game's own calendar. The travel map covers the long way round.
Towns and people
People who answer questions, trade rumours and take offence. Shops, taverns, banks, temples and guild halls, plus a watch that arrests you and a court that tries you.
Dungeons
The classic dungeons with their doors, locks, teleporters and traps, an automap that fills in as you go, and every monster from the rat to the ancient lich.
Combat
Daggerfall's own hit and damage formulas: bows both ways, knockback, armour that wears, poisons and diseases, and enemies that cast, flee and give up.
Magic
The spellbook, the spell, potion and item makers, magic items and the Daedric artifacts. Ninety of the game's ninety-one spell effects are in.
Your character
Character creation with the class questions, custom classes and the biography. After that comes levelling, skills, faction reputation, and a wagon behind your pack.
Quests
265 quests running on the game's own scripts. They get offered, argued over in the talk window, tracked in the journal, and kept across saves.
The screens
Every classic window laid out from the original art, with an enhanced set beside it. The switch is one press at the foot of the menu.
Sound and music
The game's sounds, footsteps that know what floor you're on, and its MIDI played by a synth written for this port. Your own tracks work too.

Not yet: importing classic or Daggerfall Unity saves, and mods.

How to play

Three steps, and the first two only happen once.

  1. Get Daggerfall.

    It has been free since 2009. Any of these gives you the files.

    • Steam
    • GOG
    • DaggerfallGameFiles.zip

  2. Press Play and pick your ARENA2 folder.

    The folder is inside the game install, under DF/DAGGER/ARENA2 on Steam. Choose it or drop it on the page. On a phone you can hand over the whole zip instead. You won't be asked again.

  3. Then play.

    You wake in Privateer's Hold, as everyone does. On a phone the touch controls appear on their own.

On your desktop

The browser needs no install and never will. The app is for people who want their saves to be files.

Saves you can hold

Every save is a folder of real files on your disk. It survives a cleared browser, you can back it up by copying it, and a save you copy in shows up on the load screen.

Your folder, read in place

The app reads ARENA2 straight from disk. Nothing gets copied into browser storage, every sky set loads, and there's a menu item to re-point it if your install ever moves.

Still the same deal

It ships no game data, and the only network call it makes is an optional check for a newer release. Download for Windows, macOS or Linux, or build it from source.

Good to know

Which browsers?
Any current one with WebGL2: Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. Phones get touch controls automatically.
What are the controls?
Daggerfall Unity's defaults. WASD and mouse, F6 opens the pack, F9 and F11 quick save and load, Esc for everything else. Every key can be rebound in Settings.
Where do my files and saves go?
Into your browser's storage, on your device. There's no account and no server. The desktop app keeps them as real files instead, and works fully offline.
Classic or enhanced?
Enhanced by default, but the classic screens are all there. The switch sits at the foot of the menu.
Is this Daggerfall Unity?
No, it's a port of it. Daggerfall Unity is the reference and has years more polish plus a mod scene. This is the same rules in a browser tab.
Something's wrong.
Reload the page first, since the site rebuilds several times a day. If it's a real bug, file it on the issue tracker with what you did and what happened.
