# Support Daggerfall Enhanced

Daggerfall Enhanced is free, open source, and developed independently.

Active development currently costs about **$600 per month** to sustain. That is the project's funding target.

Support directly helps cover:

- development and AI-assisted engineering tools
- multiplayer infrastructure and related services
- automated testing, builds, and release work
- mod compatibility and port maintenance
- the ongoing engineering work needed to keep the project moving

The goal is not to put features behind a paywall. The source, builds, and normal releases stay public. Sponsorship gives the project runway to keep developing.

## Monthly goal

**$600 / month**

A relatively small group of recurring supporters can cover that. For example:

| Level | Monthly support | Purpose |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Supporter | $5 | Helps cover routine project costs |
| Sustainer | $10 | Helps keep active development moving |
| Project Sponsor | $25 | Contributes directly to monthly development runway |
| Major Sponsor | $50+ | Makes a substantial contribution toward the monthly goal |

These are suggested support levels, not access tiers. No gameplay, source code, or normal releases are locked behind them.

## Support

**Ko-fi:** https://ko-fi.com/dfjs

If you want to support the project but cannot contribute financially, testing builds, reporting reproducible bugs, contributing code, documenting behavior, and helping other players are all useful.

## Why $600?

The project has reached the point where the limiting factor is no longer whether the architecture can support the work. It is the recurring cost of the tools and infrastructure used to continue that work at the current pace.

Keeping the target public makes that tradeoff clear: if the community can sustain roughly $600 per month, development has a much longer runway.
