# Patch Notes: Frame rate cap, All off, and a tidier Features page

## Frame Rate Cap
- Settings › Video › **Frame Rate Cap** now works. Pick Off, 30, 45, 60, 75, 90, 120, 144, 165, 240 or 300 frames a second. Hold Shift to jump three steps at a time.
- A cap below your screen's refresh rate saves power and heat. The game never runs faster than your screen.
- The FPS counter shows the capped rate.
- The cap is in the pause menu's Settings too.
- **Wait For Screen Refresh** now shows as always on. Browsers always wait for the screen, so it can't be turned off. Use the Frame Rate Cap to run slower.

## Features page

### All off and Restore
- A new **All off** button turns every mod and enhancement off in one go, after asking first. A row with no Off goes to Daggerfall's own setting instead: land view distance 3, dungeon walls Classic.
- Choices that are never "off", like cloud quality or grass style, stay as they are.
- **Restore** puts everything back the way you had it, even after you restart the game.
- Online, the settings the server decides stay on.

### Search
- A search box sits at the top of the page. Type any word from a feature's name or description, or a mod author's name, for example "blood", "hotbar" or "Kamer". The tiles filter as you type.
- It works in the in-game pause menu too.

### Reorganized
- Related switches are combined into single tiles. Their individual options open under the tile's arrow:
  - **Grass**: the amount (Full, Half, Quarter, Off), with the Pixel or Smooth style inside.
  - **Wind**: tree sway and wind wisps together. Each can still be switched on its own.
  - **Quick slots**: Off, Diamond or Hotbar on one bar.
  - **Blood**: Off, Light, Normal, Heavy or Abattoir, so blood finally has a single Off. Lasting marks, overkill and blood on the lens can each be switched inside.
- Two new groups:
  - **Interface**: the enhanced map, quick slots, World Tooltips, Ambient Text, Near Death Warning and Choose Guild Jobs.
  - **Sound**: Enhanced sounds, Immersive Footsteps and Combat Voices.
- Seasons of the Iliac Bay and Eye Of The Beholder moved to Sight, and "The watch defends the town" moved to The world.
- Your current settings carry over unchanged.

## Fixes
- The Enhanced environments tile now shows as off when it is off. Its options now read Off, Port sky and Dynamic Skies.
- The Morrowind assets card no longer shows the word "null".
- Switching Weapon Sheathing now updates the holster straight away while the Morrowind body is in use.

## Notes
- No features were removed. Every switch you had is still there, either on its own tile or inside a combined one.
