# Patch Notes: Enhanced Plus, Guilds and the Pack

## Enhanced Plus
- Enhanced Plus is now the only enhanced look. The UI Overhaul panel offers Classic, Enhanced Plus and GrimoireUI.
- Item borders show their rarity colour and pips everywhere: pack, loot, shop, worn slots, hotbar.
- Sigil weapons wear a rune. Their card shows the sigil's stage and a bar of its progress.
- Durability bars on weapons, armour, lights and enchanted items in the pack, loot, shop and trade.
- Double-click an item to equip or unequip it.
- **New: Lock.** Lock an item from its card or right-click menu. A locked item can't be dropped, sold or traded.
- The pack: chest, arms and legs as half panels, hover cards and a right-click menu on worn items.
- Online windows (decorator, journal, player menu, duels, invites, Guild tab) match the Enhanced Plus style.

## Controller
- Made for Enhanced Plus, with its own button-binding menu.
- Shields go on the hotbar.

## Online
- **New: Guild tags and Guild chat.** Your guild's tag shows beside your name. Chat with it on the Guild tab, or with /guild or /gu.
- Your Renown shows on your HUD with an XP bar.
- Any house can be bought. Its door lists Buy it, Go in, Who may enter and Sell it.
- See other players' torches light your way, and see and hear their hits.
- The player menu (trade, invite, inspect) opens on a key, not on a look.
- Enemies flash red when hit.

## Fixes
- Pressing Buy it twice no longer charges twice.
- Leaving a guild takes you out of Guild chat everywhere.
- A double-tap on a phone no longer presses Drop.
- Classic UI respects locked items.
- A shield no longer drops when its torch slot is empty.
- Easier to read on the Stone theme.
