# Patch Notes: Oblivion Gates (world bosses)

## The gate (online)
- Every two real hours (one game day) an Oblivion Gate opens somewhere in the Iliac Bay, at :32 past every even hour UTC.
- 15 minutes before, the sky burns: a ring marks the area on the map, chat names the nearest town, and inside it the enhanced compass points the way.
- 5 minutes before, the gate rises: two horned black pillars and a red beacon you can see from far off. Look at it for a countdown.
- It stays open 10 minutes, then seals: no one else gets in, but whoever is inside fights on. 10 minutes after that, a boss still standing unleashes Dagon's Wrath and the gate collapses.

## The Burning Court
- Step through a vortex of fire into the Deadlands: an arena over a lava sea under a burning sky, with floating islands, spires, embers, ash and red lightning.
- Valkynaz Ruhn, Warden of the Burning Gate, is huge and fights in three phases. Every attack is marked on the ground and wound up before it lands: Cleave, Ground Slam, Charge, Hellfire and Flame Nova. Move out of the marked area. Fire resistance and Shield help.
- His health grows with each player who joins, and damage is capped per player, so nobody can cheat the kill.
- Die in the court and you're thrown out in front of the gate at half health. You can go back in while it's open.

## Spoils
- When he falls he bursts, spewing loot for each player: gold, one Rare-or-better and two Magic-or-better pieces, and a Sigil Stone. Each piece bounces to rest and glows in its rarity's colour.
- Walk over a piece or activate it to take it. Anything left on the floor goes to your pack when you leave. A crash or disconnect loses nothing.
- Each kill counts toward a new **Gates closed** record on your account card and the Inspect card (registered accounts).

## Sound
- The court has its own music: a war song for each phase and a fanfare when he falls. He growls, roars and shakes the ground.

## Notes
- A full audit fixed 33 bugs before release.
