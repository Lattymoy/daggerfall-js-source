# Patch Notes: Bank of the Empire, house rooms and the guild tab

## Crash fix
- Using a Spellmaking station in your home no longer crashes the game ("m.rows.map is not a function"). The station opens the spell maker as it should.

## Online
- Every bank is now the Bank of the Empire. The Empire lends a tenth of what the old banks did (a tenth of the usual limit for your level). Interest and the year to repay are unchanged. Offline, banks are as before.
- Visitors can no longer cast spells in someone else's home: no spells readied or fired, and no magic items used ("You cannot cast spells in another's home."). A magic item refused this way loses no durability. Potions still work. Your own home is unchanged.

## Guild tab
- The Deposit, Withdraw, Invite, Found and Rename buttons now light up as soon as you type, instead of only after closing and reopening the panel.
- Member names in the roster no longer squeeze to one syllable a line while a deposit is going through; the buttons wrap below the name instead.

## Decorating your home, house or ship
- "Decoration 49" and the decorations after it - the ships' own pieces from Detailed Ships - can be placed now. Their picture showed nothing while you placed them and Place did nothing. Their pictures in the list no longer risk staying blank.
- Houses with more than one room get room tabs in the Decorate panel. Choose a room and your next placement starts in that room, even behind a closed door. "In this room" and "Built in" list the chosen room's pieces, and moving a piece starts from its own room.
- Pictures (sprites) can now face the other way: turn one more than a quarter turn while placing it and it flips.
- Crafting stations cost ten times as much: Alchemy 50,000 gold, Spellmaking 100,000, Enchanting 200,000.

## Morrowind models
- With Morrowind data attached, more items use their Morrowind look:
  - The Dwarven Thunderlock, hung on a wall and in the inventory.
  - Roleplay & Realism Items' weapons and armour (Archer's Axe, Light Flail, hauberk, chausses, spaulders, sollerets, jerkin, cuisse, helmet, boots, gloves, vambraces) - in your hands, worn, in the inventory and hung on a wall.
  - Clothing you set down in a room, which used to show as a pile of cloth.
