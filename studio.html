The studio

The pictures of the eight models the game paints for itself, as painted (the game lays Daggerfall's palette over them when it loads). Download a template, repaint it, and drop it in your folder under public/art/studio/<you>/ with the name it downloads with. The game wears it on the next load. Outlined pictures already have a studio picture (left: the studio's, right: the painted one it replaces). A picture marked glass is cut where it is clear; every other one is drawn solid. The rules are in public/art/studio/README.md; Daggerfall's own textures take the same names, and archive-sheet.html numbers them from your own copy of the game.
