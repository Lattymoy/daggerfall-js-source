# Patch Notes: UX backlog, shared keys and the Morrowind card

## Menu and settings
- **Skip Start Video** (Settings › Interface, off by default) opens straight onto the main menu.
- **Sync from server** (Online page) copies the online rules into your offline settings, with **Undo**.

## Controls
- **One key, several actions.** Binding a key that's in use now offers **Use for both** (B in the classic menu). Shared keys show green.
- On the enhanced Controls page, the save button is now called **Confirm**, and the ✕ and right-click clear a key straight away.
- The "key in use" question stays at the top of the page, with both rows highlighted.
- Keys Controls can't change (HUD toggles, Transport's horse and cart) are listed at its bottom, and each mod's Features tile lists its keys.

## Character creation
- Hover an attribute to see what it does.
- **Export** and **Import** custom classes, as a file or through the clipboard.
- The difficulty dagger is back on the class builder.
- The creation screens fit a 16:9 screen without scrolling.

## Shops
- Repairs show when they'll be ready: "DONE" / "N days" on the classic counter, a countdown on the enhanced one.
- A part-stack you put back on the shelf rejoins its stack instead of making a second row.
- In the classic shop, Ctrl is read from the click itself, so a Ctrl-click always asks how many.

## World
- The private property question has clickable **Yes** and **No** buttons.
- A container that belongs to someone else is labelled **Private property** in its own colour, and shows **Searched** once you've opened it (enhanced skin).

## Morrowind assets
- The card is now at the top of the Features page, with just **Attach data** and **Remove data**.
- Attached files are always used, offline and online. To turn the Morrowind body off, remove the data.
- Removed from the card: the On/Off switch, the look-lag option (the arms now stay fixed to the screen), and the mesh viewer and data inspector links.
