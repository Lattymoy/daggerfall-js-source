# Patch Notes: Bloodmoon's Werewolf and Shadow Fang

## Werewolves
- **New: Bloodmoon's werewolf.** With Morrowind assets on and Bloodmoon attached, you become Bloodmoon's own 3D werewolf when you transform, in first and third person.
- Other players with Bloodmoon attached see your werewolf too. Until it loads for them, they see the Eye Of The Beholder werewolf.
- While your werewolf loads, and while your own body loads when you turn back, you see the classic hands, or the Eye Of The Beholder sprite in third person.
- Turning back gives you your own body and gear again.
- The werewolf holds no weapon, torch or lantern, and shows no spellcasting.
- Without Bloodmoon nothing changes: you're the Eye Of The Beholder werewolf with the classic claws.
- The wereboar has no Morrowind model and is unchanged.
- This is new and hasn't been tried on real Bloodmoon files yet. If your werewolf won't load, the Morrowind card names the file it stopped at.

## Titles
- **New title: Shadow Fang**, in black and crimson, with a snarling wolf's-head glyph. It's SirMcMobdon's: press it on the account card to wear it.
- Shadow Fang's werewolf has its own look: darker fur, crimson through the edges of the fur, and red eyes where Bloodmoon's textures show them. Everyone with Bloodmoon attached sees it.
- Tabby is now a Developer.
- Flylighter is now a Disciple.
- SirMcMobdon is no longer an Apostle.

## Behind the scenes
- Everyone online is disconnected once when this update goes live, and reconnects.
- Title updates now wait for the online server to update first, so a new title can't stop anyone connecting.
