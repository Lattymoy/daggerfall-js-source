# Patch Notes: One Escape, and six field fixes

## Controls
- One Escape opens the pause menu. It used to take two, because the browser kept the first press to free the mouse.
- Desktop app: closing a menu with Escape puts you straight back into mouse-look. In a browser tab, click or press a key to take the mouse back - the browser does not let a page do it on Escape.

## Crime
- Offline, guards no longer hit you while the court and prison screens are up. A blow there could kill you mid-sentence or start a second trial that lost your release, so the crime never cleared.
- Still Daggerfall's rule: a sentence only restores half your standing in the region, and a bad name there can still bring the watch after you.

## Inventory
- At 1366x768 and smaller, the wagon and your own storage fit beside the pack, and every item in them can be reached.
- Roleplay & Realism: the weight penalty now starts at 75% of the carry weight your pack shows, weight-allowance gear included - not at your bare strength limit.

## Main quest
- Mantellan Crux: a door inside a dungeon only takes you outside if it is really the dungeon's exit. The Crux's own teleports work again.

## Online
- Players above level 30 can cast healing and buffs on party members again. Their spells land at level 30 strength.

## Skins
- The female Acrobat skin is no longer short from the front three-quarter angle.
