# Patch Notes: Daggerfall Online

## A new name
- Daggerfall Enhanced is now **Daggerfall Online** - on the website, in the game, on the home-screen app, in the desktop app, on the pause screen and in About.
- A new logo: it flies in at the end of the intro, heads the main menu and sits at the top of Settings.
- The intro's music is titled "Daggerfall Online Main Theme". Same recording, same timing.

## Desktop app
- The app, its window and its downloads are called Daggerfall Online (`DaggerfallOnline-...-setup.exe`, `-portable.exe`, the AppImage and the dmg).
- Installed copies update themselves as before. Your saves, settings and ARENA2 folder stay where they are.
- On macOS the new download is "Daggerfall Online.app". It uses the same saves as the old app, so you can delete "Daggerfall Enhanced.app" once you have moved over.

## Also
- Exported saves now download as `DaggerfallOnline-Saves.zip`. Class files you exported before still import.
- The Enhanced Plus UI keeps its name. The Patreon and Discord links are unchanged.
