# Patch Notes: The UI Pass

Inventory, hotbar and HUD, reworked (the enhanced skin).

## Inventory slots
- Item sprites are sized to their slot: trimmed, drawn in whole pixels where they can be, never blurred or crushed. A longsword fills its slot; a gem is no longer a speck.
- The rarity outline is the slot's own border (grid, worn slots, accessory sockets, hotbar, quickslot diamond). No more icon inside an icon.
- Stacks show their count on the slot.

## Hotbar
- Spells show their real Daggerfall icons.
- Any item can go on the bar: armour, clothing and jewellery are put on or taken off in one press; books, maps, the spellbook, food, tents and the rest are used as from the pack.

## Status widget
- Buffs, debuffs and needs left the bottom of the screen for a column of square icons at the left edge, above the quickslot diamond.
- Spells show their own icons, set powers their rune in the set's colour, and there are new pixel icons for hunger, thirst, sleep, wet, hot, cold, stiff, drunk, poison and disease.
- The frame says what it is: green buff, red debuff, amber need, red need that is costing you. Time left at the foot; ending spells blink.
- Poisons and diseases show on the HUD for the first time, once they take hold.
- It never covers the chat, the diamond or the crosshair. It wraps into columns, sits beside the diamond on a sideways phone, and folds extras into "+N".

## XP bar
- The Renown bar is full height with your XP inside it ("5,420 / 13,800 XP").
- On phones the vitals show just their percentages (no more "MAGICKA70%").

## Audit fixes
- Sprites stay pixel-sharp at 125% and 150% zoom and on phones; an open inventory refits after a resize or rotation.
- Hotbar: quest items work from a slot; a camp the ground refuses no longer flashes as used; spell icons fit their own ring.
- Status: party mates' buffs count as buffs, each with its own timer; the column stays out of the compass and touch buttons.
- Items on the ground show their true colours.
