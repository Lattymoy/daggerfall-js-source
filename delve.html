Delve lab - curated blocks

A curated block is a real interior block of your own game files with things taken out, things added and its encounters placed by hand (bible/03-World/Delve-II.md, section 8). The lab opens it in the game's dungeon bench: walk it, put things at your feet from the panel, rebuild, and export the record into src/assets/delve/.
