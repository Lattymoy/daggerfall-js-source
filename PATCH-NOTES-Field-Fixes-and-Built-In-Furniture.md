# Patch Notes: Field fixes and built-in furniture

## Quests
- Sharing a quest works again. It was refused with "did not match your own copy".
- Your quests show on F5's pause menu, as they do on Tab.
- Guard the Guild (Mages Guild, online): the watch starts about a minute after you arrive and lasts five minutes.

## Homes
- **New: Built in.** A fourth tab in the Decorate panel lists the room's own furniture. Take any piece out, or put it back, free, one at a time or all at once. A piece holding items stays. Online, visitors see the room as you left it. Selling the house puts everything back.
- Online: a house for sale offers itself at its door in any mode but Steal, not only Info. This is why Enhanced Plus couldn't buy one.
- Armour can be hung on the wall, like weapons.
- Hung weapons show their own picture again.
- Enhanced skins: your own storage (the ship's chest, your house's cupboards, placed storage) takes items again, with your pack beside it.

## Online
- Dungeon rest ambushes belong to the whole group: everyone sees and fights the same foe, and its loot is shared.
- Oblivion Gates: the beacon shows from far away, and every player gets the same gate.
- Others see your werewolf or wereboar the moment you change.

## Combat and characters
- Bows play one sound per shot.
- Morrowind view: an empty hand is never the one in use, and H only swaps between two held weapons.
- Morrowind view: you see yourself change into the beast.
- Vampires: no more sun damage. By day your stats are 20 lower, at night 20 higher. Holy ground and feeding still apply.

## World
- Shopkeepers no longer appear as lamp posts.
- A swimmer too heavy to stay afloat is lifted onto the shore at the coast instead of being stuck on the sea floor.
