# Patch Notes: The ghosts of Daggerfall, shared

## Monsters (online)
- The ghosts and wraiths that haunt Daggerfall's streets at night are now shared. Everyone nearby sees the same ones, anyone can fight them, and they go after every player. Before, each player had their own that nobody else could see, so everyone had to kill their own.
- If you leave the area or die while they are after you, your ghosts pass to the nearest player instead of vanishing.
- Each player still under the curse brings their own ghosts, so a bigger group meets more of them - and can now fight them together.
- Monsters from your other quests stay yours alone (or your party's, for a quest you shared with it).
