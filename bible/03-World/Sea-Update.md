# The Sea

The way into the sea's pages. The sea is several vendored mods ported from their assemblies, and the port's own systems
built over them. Every mod here is on by default, registered (settings, Features, credits,
`01-Overview/Mod-Registry.md`) and placed in the online lane (`src/systems/online/onlineLane.js`).

## How it fits together

| System | What it gives | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Iliac Puddle No More (jet082) | a carved sea with depth, seafloor life, fish, foes, sunken loot and swimming; the continent's sea carved the same way | `Deep-Waters.md` |
| There's a Hole in the Bottom of the Ocean (jet082) | seafloor pits leading to drowned dungeons | `Ocean-Holes.md` |
| Come Sail Away (RedRoryOTheGlen) | boats bought, placed and sailed under oars or sail | `Come-Sail-Away.md` |
| Warm Ashes - Ships (Kamer) | pirate ambushes on sea voyages, raiders seen on the Overworld | `Warm-Ashes-Ships.md` |
| The sea fight | broadsides, boarding, plunder, the Bay's ships and navies, ship life, crews | `Naval-Combat.md` |
| The sea's second refit | shot kinds, the ram, grapples, looks, the captain's stars, metal refits | `Naval-Refit.md` |
| The captain's sea | crew veterancy, standing with crowns, letters of marque, the spyglass, the board, the exchange | `Captains-Sea.md` |
| Holdings | the Fleet's ledger, quays and docking, crew roles | `Holdings.md` |
| Shipyards | the shipmaster, the crew hall, the beacon tower | `Shipyards.md` |
| Passage | riding a friendly ship to a known port | `Passage.md` |
| Ship refit | furnished player ships | `Ship-Refit.md` |
| The Eltheric Ocean | the procedural sea past the Veil | `Eltheric-Ocean.md` |

## Rules

- All mods attached are compatible and implemented 1:1, on by default, with what online needs chosen carefully (Mac).
- No bugs, no band aids; the water overhaul covers puddles and one-square town water
  (`07-Rendering/Water-Arc.md`).
- With Come Sail Away off there are no player boats and no sea fight.

## Daggerfall Unity

Daggerfall's sea is a flat sheet of water and DFU has no sailing. Each mod's own page names its departures.
