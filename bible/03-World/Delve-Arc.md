# Delve - Dungeons a Player Can Read

Tools that make Daggerfall's dungeons legible without changing their layouts: a look round that lights what is near,
the echo of what a lever moved, the way out on the compass, quest guidance tiers, Detect Treasure on searched finds,
and dungeon sizes. Delve II (`Delve-II.md`) adds encounters. Every tool except Detect's finds is an Enhanced row on the
Features home with its own switch (`src/systems/features.js`).

| Tool | Row | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Look round | `dungeon-sense` | On (secrets off) |
| Echo and examine | `dungeon-echoes` | On |
| Way out | `dungeon-way-out` | On |
| Guidance tiers | `quest-guidance` | Journal |
| Medium dungeons | `medium-dungeons` | Off |
| Dungeon sizes as online | `world-dungeon-sizes` | Off offline, forced on online |

## How it works

- **Look round** (`src/systems/dungeon/dungeonSense.js`). Asking for Info mode underground (its key, the HUD's cycle,
  the pad) is a look round; the ask is counted where the press is read, so it fires even when Info is already on. What
  lights is what the plaque would name, within `SENSE_M` (8 m) and in plain sight, at most `SENSE_MAX` nearest first,
  for `SENSE_HOLD_S` (6 s). Kinds (a thing to work, a way through, a find, a secret) differ in colour and in form, so a
  colour-blind eye can tell them. The opt-in secrets tier lights walls and doors only a chain moves. The glow is the
  professions' (`src/render/nodeGlow.js`).
- **Echo** (`src/systems/dungeon/dungeonEcho.js`). When a press sets a mover going more than `ECHO_NEAR_M` (3 m) away
  and out of sight, the popup says once what moved and which way ("Stone grinds somewhere below you, to the west."). It
  goes on the held map as an echo mark (at most 8) until the player sees it within `ECHO_FIND_M` (12 m), then lights.
  In Info mode the plaque over an action object says which way its chain works, walked without playing it.
- **Way out** (`src/systems/wayOut.js`). A breadth-first field from the way in over the cells the player has stood on
  (`src/systems/automap/automap.js` trail, gaps up to `TRAIL_FILL_M` filled), steps blocked by the dungeon's own
  geometry, walked teleporters as one-way steps. The enhanced compass's arrow points at the farthest visible cell
  along it (`WAY_LOOK_CELLS`), so it turns the corners the player turned. With no walked way it points straight at the
  way in. It hides within `WAY_HERE_M` (3 m) of the way in.
- **Guidance tiers** (`src/systems/quest/questGuidance.js` `GUIDANCE_TIERS`). Journal is the default. Town rings the
  building the journal names on the held town map and points the compass at it. Exact also marks every quest resource
  standing in the dungeon or building (never a hidden one) on the map and compass; it can spoil a search, so it is off
  by default and enhanced only.
- **Detect Treasure** finds a searched object's find (`src/scenes/shared.js` `searchedNearbyRecord`). An unsearched
  searchable holds nothing yet, so it is not treasure.
- **Medium dungeons** (`src/world/smallerDungeons.js` `generateMediumDungeon`). A dungeon of more than 8 blocks is
  regenerated by DFU's GenerateSmallerDungeon law, seeded on its map id: two interior blocks side by side and the six
  border blocks that close them. An interior block is never laid twice (`distinctInterior`). Smaller Dungeons wins when
  both are on; DFU's own switch stays DFU's.
- **Online sizes** (`onlineDungeonSize`). Online every dungeon has the world's own size, one seeded draw on its map id:
  small 1, medium 2, full 1 (`ONLINE_DUNGEON_SIZES`). A re-laid dungeon's room is keyed by its size
  (`src/net/online.js` `roomKeyFor`).

## Rules

- Make dungeons less esoteric, give them logical interaction moments and a way to detect interactables (the owner).
- The dungeon's layout stays Daggerfall's; only the size tiers re-lay it.
- Large, medium and small all play online: the world mixes sizes (Mac).
- Saves and quests: a quest frozen at a size keeps it. The size stamps append past DFU's NotSet, Disabled and Enabled:
  3 medium, `ONLINE_DUNGEONS_STATE` 4, `MEDIUM_DISTINCT_STAMP` 5 (a save stamp only). A save loaded at another size
  stands at the start and leaves its world record unapplied. A running quest whose dungeon moved has its markers
  enumerated again at load (`src/systems/quest/questRepair.js` `relayMovedLayouts`, `relayOnlineDungeons`).
- Tamriel: the tools read the dungeon's own layout and the player's trail, never a place.

## Daggerfall Unity

The size law is DFU's `GenerateSmallerDungeon` with its block pools and DFRandom seed. The plaque's action band is
asked before the door band, as World Tooltips does. Departures: the medium size and the online sizes, the distinct
interior blocks, and the legibility tools, which DFU does not have.

## Open

- Indoors, a shared quest's foe owned by a party mate's client is not marked by the Exact tier.
- A moved medium dungeon's online room is named by its size, not its draw, so room memory written before the distinct
  fix is read into the new layout (Mac to decide).
