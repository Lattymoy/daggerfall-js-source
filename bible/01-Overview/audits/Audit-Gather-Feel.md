# AUDIT GATHER-FEEL (2026-10-13) - the gathering heard, felt, cracked and shared, audited

Mac, of GATHER-FEEL and GATHER-FEEL II (`06-Systems/Professions-Arc.md` sections 44 and 45, PR #868): **"audit
everything"**. This page is the audit of both - the ear and the last blow, BLOW-SHAKE, VEIN-CRACKS and PEER-ACTS - and
its record. What the audit changed is written into the record's sections as they now stand, each marked `AUDIT
GATHER-FEEL <id>`; this page is the findings, each with its verdict.

## How it was run

Four lanes, each a cold adversarial reader of a FROZEN snapshot (a git worktree at 5c345face - Home.md's DO NOT FIX
WHILE THE VERIFIER IS READING), told to reproduce every finding (a script through the real modules, a surviving mutant,
a number) before reporting it:

| Lane | Over | How |
|---|---|---|
| F | GATHER-FEEL's ear and last blow, BLOW-SHAKE (`systems/gatherSounds.js`, `systems/chopAct.js`, `systems/mineAct.js`, `systems/gatherBlow.js`'s feel, `scenes/gatherHost.js`'s act frame) | the real host over the real kinds (`test/gatherRig.mjs`); sweeps of the frame's length; a second act begun under a last blow; the dungeon left and the switch shut under one; the ear's allocations measured; every clip baked again byte for byte; the type checker over the JSDoc |

Nothing a player gets crashed or lost anything. One HIGH: every mining act's start blanked its pixel's flats for a
frame. The rest were a blow's landing on three clocks, places the floating origin left behind, a peer hearing what the
gatherer had taken back, chunks lying off the ground, and laws no pin could fail.
