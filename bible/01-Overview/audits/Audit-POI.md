# AUDIT POI (2026-10-15) - the POI Tool, audited: its store, the game, the editor, the panels, glTF, the UV editor and the packs

Mac, of the POI Tool arc (`06-Systems/POI-Editor-Arc.md`, POI-1 - POI-6): **"Audit everything. Ensure perfection."**
This page is the audit of the whole arc - the store and the law it shares, the game that stands the POIs, the editor's
core and its host in the world, the panels and their words, glTF in and out, the UV editor and the library's picture,
the packs, the diff and the shelf entry, and the record and its pins - and its findings, each with its verdict. What the
audit changed is written into the arc's page as it now stands, each change marked `AUDIT POI` with the finding's id; in
the code each fix carries `// AUDIT POI <ID>` where it stands.
