## The kept graph (CACHE 1-6, LAW 2-3, HOST 3-4)

**Its memory (C1, L2, H3).** A graph of node and chain objects, two typed arrays a chain: the network has a junction
every 4.2 pixels, so headers were most of it - 20 MB after one trip, 179 MB after the network was walked, 368 MB with
the slot before it. Nothing owned it. **Fixed**: flat typed arrays (`travelRoute.js:"function roadGraphFor"`), 15 MB
of arrays after the 992 trips; kept on the ground the host owns (`routeGround`'s `roadKeep`), one graph at a time, dropped whole
past ROAD_GRAPH_MAX_PIXELS. A caller with no keep keeps nothing.

**Its key (C2, C5, L3).** The second slot held only a graph no key could match again, and a reader other than the
world's was known by its identity alone. **Fixed**: one graph a keep, and only the world's reader (its install's
version) is kept.

**A plan sliced across a change (C3).** A walk left mid-chain by a slice, the layer laid again, went round a ring it was
not on for ever. **Fixed**: a plan whose network moved under it is planned again as it lies (pin: 11 slices, where the
walk went on for 142).

**Installs (C6, H4).** Every region that lands moves the version, and the next plan walks afresh. **OPEN**: the first
minutes of a session plan cold; a tile's own stamp could keep the rest.
