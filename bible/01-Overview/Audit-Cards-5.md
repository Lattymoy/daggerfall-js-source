## What held

- **The rules:** 200,000 games broke no invariant; neither the view nor the reveal's events leak a hidden card (only the
  last turn's unveil names one, by rule); every card and every location does what its text says (82 of 82, 22 of 22),
  A1, A2 and A7 apart. The engine imports only pure modules - no `Math.random`, no clock, no crypto.
- **The faces:** every reading text passes WCAG AA; the back is clean; Port-Doctrine holds - no raster, no copied asset,
  no font file, every emblem hand-typed paths of a generic pictogram, none a trace.
- **The items:** the binder is refused at every door driven; a card keeps its `card` through every split and copy; the
  gift is never given twice; rarity 20 minted no card and no binder in 2.1M rolls.
- **The page:** the rail's four cases (Loot rarity on or off, a binder or none), the reset of a new visit, and neither
  width scrolling sideways. Section 26's every claim and the pages that cite it checked true; Audit-Cards-4's spot-checked.
- **The gold tables:** no gold CREATED in 1,950 evenings - the per-account ledger, the relay's conservation, the whole
  and every paid amount held throughout, with no duplicate receipt; every AUDIT CARDS-4 fix held on its own replay; the
  top-up kept the books refused, raced (48 runs) and reused. What was STRANDED (48 rows, 68,414 gold - 0.26% of all
  staked) was NEW-2 and NEW-3.
