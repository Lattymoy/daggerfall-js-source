# INCIDENT 2026-09-01 - the day the textures broke twice

Two separate breakages hit the live site in one afternoon, and the
record matters more than most because both classes are invisible to
the suite: 5,600+ tests were green while players saw a broken game.

## The first break: the other account's afternoon

A second account ran an Enhanced Environments arc (EE1-EE8) plus a
tile-array probe against the game's own shaders and texture system.
Its own commit titles narrate the failure ("the black screen", "the
void was an unsized texture format", "the black world"), and its
partial self-reverts left MW/EE residue in nine src files and deleted
`Audit-44.md` as collateral. RESOLUTION: a solid revert - one
append-only commit restoring the last fully-gated tree byte-identical
(`edab46e`, PR #47), everything recoverable from history.

## The second break: ours, riding Wave A

The site was still broken at the restored state, which meant the
breakage was OURS. A three-lane forensic fleet (each lane required to
produce the exact wrong value, not a suspicion) found two independent
defects, and cleared a third suspect with certainty:

1. **THE PALETTE CLOBBER** (the whole-game symptom: weapons gold,
   caves and exteriors mis-colored). `PRIS00I0.IMG` - the A3 prison
   screen's art - is one of the SIX palettized IMGs, and
   `ImgFile._readPalette` writes the embedded palette INTO the palette
   object it is handed. The preload took the host `deps` bag whole, so
   the session's shared ART_PAL rode in and was overwritten at boot;
   every texture decoded after it wore the prison screen's colors.
   The law was already written in `titleScreen.js:44-46` (U18/17k) -
   the new site simply did not know it. FIX: the preload mints its own
   `DFPalette`; PINS: the behavioural own-palette test and a
   palettized-IMG site sweep (`test/incident_texture.test.js`).

2. **THE TERRAIN WINTER TILESET** (snow under deserts). Classic
   Daggerfall starts on the 4th of Morning Star - WINTER - and A1
   made the calendar real, lighting a path the port had never
   rendered. The terrain keyed its tileset on the GROUND-PLANE member
   (`DaggerfallGroundPlane.cs:83`) instead of the terrain's own
   (`TerrainMaterialProvider.GetClimateInfo`, :120-134), which keys on
   the climate's `GroundArchive` field and refuses the winter +1 in
   Desert-base climates. Three of ten climates built archives DFU
   never builds. FIX: `getTerrainGroundArchive`, verbatim with the
   Desert guard; PIN: the ten-climate/two-season truth table.

3. **CLEARED: the emissive rework.** The gold could not mechanically
   come from emission (the FPS weapon path touches no emission
   uniform), and reverting the subtract law would have re-broken
   auto-emissive flats while leaving the real defect live. The
   forensic lane's refusal to revert the innocent change is the
   method working.

## How it was isolated

A live-site bisect with the owner's eyes as the oracle: deploy the
pre-Wave-A checkpoint (PR #48), owner confirms clean, defects isolated
to Wave A, fixes land with pins, the checkpoint superseded WHOLE by an
`-s ours` merge (never three-way-mixed - a plain merge of a
revert-commit into the fixed branch silently re-reverts every file the
branch did not touch). The repaired campaign returned as PR #49.

## Standing lessons

1. **The palette is shared and mutable, and any palettized-IMG load
   can destroy it.** The site sweep in `incident_texture.test.js` is
   the guard; any new palettized-IMG consumer must mint its own
   palette or extend that sweep.
2. **A first-ever-rendered path is a release risk however lawful the
   code.** Winter-at-boot was CORRECT 1:1 behavior and still broke the
   site, because the winter texture path had never once drawn. Any
   change that lights a never-rendered path gets the owner's eyes
   before merge - the suite cannot see pixels.
3. **The bisect discipline**: known-good checkpoints are only known
   because every merge was gated; deploy-and-eyeball converges in
   O(log n) only when the checkpoints exist. Keep merging through
   gated PRs.
4. **Never plain-merge a revert commit into the branch that carries
   the fix.** `-s ours` or a tree-restore; the three-way silently
   prefers the revert wherever the fix branch is quiet.
