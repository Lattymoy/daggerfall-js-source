## The one-sentence version

The **law** is ported to a standard that is genuinely rare — table after table diffs byte-exact against the C#, including DFU's own bugs and typos — and the **wiring between that law and the hosts that must feed it** is where essentially every remaining defect lives.

## The overall picture

**Where the port is genuinely at parity.** The data and formula layers are as close to 1:1 as a port of this size gets. Across the lanes, roughly two dozen large reference tables were diffed mechanically and came back byte-exact: all 905 `Arch3dPatch` entries, all 63 `MobileEnemy` rows, the 45×20 encounter tables, all 133 `SongFiles` members and 39 playlists, all 74 `SoundClips` indices, the 682-cell `borderRegions` table, the 22 `LootChanceMatrix` rows, 171 `defaults.ini` keys, all 13 building-name tables, the 120-entry class-question table, all 91 classic effect keys, the 24-effect enchantment catalogue, all 82 quest action templates in DFU's declaration order. Several lanes independently found the port reproducing DFU's **own bugs** — the `4 -1` missing-comma typo in the Orc Warlord's attack frames, the keys/keys2 partner-draw bug in the region simulation, the seconds/minutes mix in `Clock.GetTravelTimeInSeconds`, the desync-on-failed-gate in the spell reader. That is the signature of a port that read the source rather than reimplemented the behaviour, and it is the strongest single fact in this report.

**Where it is not.** Almost every remaining defect is one shape: **a correct, complete law whose caller does not deliver it.** The repo has already named this class itself — the Ledger's 2026-08-31 entry opens with "six times in one session a call reached a seam that was not there" — and the 32 lanes found it in twenty-six of thirty-two. It appears as an option a callee destructures that no host passes (`ctx.hurtSelf`, `onExteriorWater`, `deps.inside`, `travelMap`, `freeTavernRooms`, `host.spawnCityGuards`), as a producer whose consumer is conditionally absent (`maxMagickaModifier` after a load-from-title), as a value read at the wrong time (region index captured at boot, `LiveSpeed` frozen at spawn, `nowMinutes` hard-zero), as a shape mismatch between a faithful callee and its real callers (`clipSweepTimes`, `w.input`, the impact-flash uploader), and as a global registration taken and never returned (the dungeon's death presenter). The consequences are disproportionate to the code involved: a bow that does nothing above ground, a Levitate that kills you, a Thieves Guild you cannot join, gold that duplicates itself, and every male character addressed as "Ma'am".

**The second theme is presentation-layer drift on the default configuration.** The enhanced skin ships as the default and is not the ported surface. Five classic HUD components sit below its early return, two of its window doors do not answer the host's overlay contract and throw on a keypress, two reachable chargen stages have no view, and one of its stylesheets — carrying unscoped `*`/`html`/`body`/`button` rules — is injected on the *classic* skin. A player booting the shipping build meets a materially less complete game than the classic-skin code proves the port can render.

**The third is that the repo's own accounting has been outrun by its own work.** `Port-Completion-Analysis.md` (snapshot 2026-08-22, re-verified 08-27) measures 88,640 JS loc across 315 files and projects 45,000–55,000 loc remaining, "roughly two thirds through by volume". `src/` today is **164,220 loc across 477 files** — it has nearly doubled since that measurement, and most of the page's "never touched" table is struck. Its surviving "small-residue tail" is stale in two of six items: `CfaFile` and `HeadBobber` both ship (verified). Conversely the Ledger's honest headline — "Section C is spent … the routed gap list is not where the next slice comes from" — is correct, and this audit confirms why: **the remaining work is not a list of unported features, it is a list of unconnected ones**, and a routed-gap ledger is structurally unable to see that class.

**Deliberate departures, counted as departures.** The voxel character engine, the enhanced skin and visuals, and the Morrowind lane are design choices, not gaps, and each is internally faithful to whatever reference it does have. Two things are worth saying about them anyway, without recategorising them: the voxel rig is **editor-only** in every game host today, so the departure is not actually shipping; and the enhanced skin, because it is the *default*, is the surface through which the classic-lane gaps above are experienced. Also ledgered and correctly excluded: the Perlin/UnityRandom substitutions, the two-camera sky rig, the byte-buffer file runtime, the mod/asset-injection system (Ledger C), and the removed road system.

**Verification quality.** The mechanically gated half of the documentation is exact — the lane independently reproduced the 151-entry open-flag list, the 5110/529 test pins and the module counts. The ungated half is drifting, with two claims false in ways a reader would act on. Two structural verification holes remain and matter for any 1:1 claim: 199 of the suite's tests are ARENA2-gated and do not run in CI at all, so the corpus half of the suite is unexercised there; and the port-original render modules (frustum, far ring, precipitation, enhanced sky) have **no parity oracle by construction** — their tests pin the port's own recorded laws, and in one case pin GLSL as text, which is how a dead-but-present star-field layer passed every check.

## Overall
Structurally, this is a port whose *law* is at or very near 1:1 and whose *wiring* is not. Across 32 lanes, roughly two dozen large reference tables diffed byte-exact against the C# — all 905 Arch3dPatch entries, all 63 MobileEnemy rows, the 45x20 encounter tables, 133 SongFiles and 39 playlists, the 682-cell borderRegions table, 171 defaults.ini keys, all 91 classic effect keys, all 82 quest actions in declaration order — and multiple lanes found the port faithfully reproducing DFU's own bugs and typos, which is the signature of reading the source rather than reimplementing the behaviour. Against that, essentially every remaining defect is one shape the repo has already named for itself: a correct, complete law whose caller does not deliver it — an option no host passes, a value captured at the wrong time, a global registration taken and never returned, a callee whose shape does not match its real callers. Twenty-six of thirty-two lanes found instances, and the consequences are wildly out of proportion to the code involved (a bow that does nothing above ground, a Levitate that kills you, two guilds that cannot be joined, gold that duplicates, every male PC addressed "Ma'am"). Layered on top is a presentation problem: the enhanced skin ships as the default and is not the ported surface, so the shipping build is materially less complete than the classic-skin code proves the port can render. The repo's own accounting is honest but has been outrun — Port-Completion-Analysis measures 88,640 loc and projects "two thirds done" against a tree that is now 164,220 loc, and the Ledger's own headline that its routed gap list is "spent" is correct precisely because the remaining work is not unported features but unconnected ones, which a routed-gap ledger cannot see.
