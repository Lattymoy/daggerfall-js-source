# Patch Notes: Oblivion Gates - the Warden reforged

Changes after the first live fights, from your reports.

## Fixed
- **The gate and the Burning Court are visible.** No more invisible floor.
- **You can leave after the fight.** A portal of fire rises where Valkynaz Ruhn falls. Walk through it to go home.
- **Soul Trap works on him.** Kill him while it lasts and his soul fills an empty gem.
- **Your gear doesn't wear out on him**, Mehrunes' Razor and Cast When Strikes weapons included.

## The fight
- **Three phases, each with its own attacks:**
  - **The Warden**: Cleave, Ground Slam, Charge.
  - **The Burning Court**: Hellfire and the new **Meteor of Oblivion** leave the floor burning. Flame Nova, and the new **Crushing Leap** at anyone far away.
  - **Dagon's Champion**: the new **Spokes of Dagon**, four lanes of fire. Stand between them.
- He leaps to the centre at each phase change, and the boss bar names the phase. A ring and arrow under him show where he stands and faces.
- **He hits harder**: a share of your health plus a flat amount. A third hit will finish you, and so will two Novas or Meteors.
- **No regeneration in the Deadlands.** Healing spells and potions still work.

## Spoils
- Each drop shows its own picture, with a line of its rarity's colour.
- Your spoils are yours alone and can't be collected twice on two devices or tabs.

## Over the gate
- **The sky burns like the Dread event**: crimson from the announcement, deeper as it opens, clearing as it collapses, fading out about 12 km away.
- A red storm strikes around the gate, so you can follow it there.
- **The boss music is much louder and more intense**, driving harder each phase.

## More fixes
- Players who join and leave no longer make the boss tougher.
- If everyone leaves and returns, he's as wounded as they left him.
- Loading new land does less work with World of Daggerfall on.
- Over a dozen smaller fixes from a full audit.
- Reload the game after the update to fight.
