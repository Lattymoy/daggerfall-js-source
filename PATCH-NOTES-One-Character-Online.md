# Patch Notes: One character online, backups and balance

## Online
- You can be online as one character at a time. Going online in a new tab, window or device takes the old one offline - the newest wins, on any device signed in to your account and in every tab of one browser.
- The old tab says so and shows **Play online here** under the chat (even with the chat hidden). Press it to switch back.
- An offline tab earns no Renown, its sigils and sets sleep, and closing it no longer overwrites your saves.
- Your party survives a tab switch, and no longer disappears when your connection blips while nobody else is online.
- Reload any game tab left open from before this update.

## Backups
- A save whose backup is newer than the copy on your device now says **Newer backup**.
- On the Load screen, **Restore backup** brings it back (press twice). The older copy is replaced only once the backup has arrived.
- **Back up again** asks twice before putting an older save over a newer backup.

## Phones
- Character creation fits phones: every attribute can be raised on the stats page, upright or on its side, and Continue stays on screen on large touch screens.

## Balance
- Stamina drains a quarter slower from walking, running, climbing, swimming, jumping, weapon swings, carrying too much and charging on horseback.
- Weapons and armour wear 40% slower.
