# Patch Notes: Party buffs, home stations and controllers

## Party
- Each party member's card now shows their active spell effects in a row under their bars: the spell's icon, the rounds left, and harmful effects outlined in red. A buff you cast on a friend shows on their card and counts down on their clock.
- A heal a party member receives floats up as a green "+N" off their card. Rests do not float numbers.
- On the Enhanced HUD, a heal you receive floats up as a green "+N" just under the crosshair, beside the damage numbers.
- Party members are now green marks on the compass, on the classic and Enhanced HUDs (the Large HUD's compass shows none). Nearby members are marked where they stand. Outdoors, members elsewhere are marked toward where they are. An invisible party member is not marked.
- New setting, "Rest with my party" (Features, in the Other players card). Turn it off to rest on your own. The party then rests without you, and you are never pulled into their rest. If the leader turns it off, everyone rests on their own. A rest on your own cannot be taken right beside party members who are resting; rest a little farther away.

## Spells on other players
- A caster-only buff (most healer spells, including Shield) no longer goes off on yourself the moment you ready it while a party member is nearby. Aim at the party member and click to cast it on them, or aim anywhere else to cast it on yourself. With only other players nearby, it casts on you at once, as before.
- Buffs from other players now appear with your buffs, not your debuffs.
- Aiming a readied spell at a player shows "Cast [spell] on [name]".
- An area spell that reaches several party members says one line naming all of them. It reaches your party first.
- You can now cast safe spells on players outside your party: Heal, Regenerate, Spell Absorption, Cure, Fortify Attribute, Shield, the resistances, Jumping and Water Breathing. A new "Spells from strangers" setting (on by default) lets you refuse them. A stranger's spell only lands from someone near you, and a stranger's Cure Disease leaves a vampirism or lycanthropy infection alone.
- A healing or protective spell is never cast on your duel opponent.

## Quest sharing
- Guild and temple quests that send you to large dungeons can be shared again. They no longer fail with "This quest is too complex to share."
- If a share fails because two players are on different versions of the game, the message now says so and tells you to reload.
- A quest your party keeps in sync no longer repeats the same refusal every few seconds.
- Refusal messages are worded properly.

## Items and trade
- The Enhanced item card now lists a magic item's powers, including Daggerfall's own magic items and ones from the item maker. Unidentified items say "Powers unknown."
- In player trades, hovering over an item shows its powers, for your items and the other player's. The trade detail lists them too, artifacts included.
- An offer too large to send in one trade is now refused with a message. Before, the trade silently timed out.
- New "Post in chat" button on the item card and right-click menu: it posts the item's name, damage or armour, and powers to your open chat tab.

## Homes and ships
- A placed piece of furniture in your home, house or ship can be made a crafting station: Alchemy (5,000 gold), Spellmaking (10,000) or Enchanting (20,000). The fee is paid once and is not refunded. Using the station opens that craft's maker, with no guild membership needed. Find it in the decorator's "In this room" view.
- Unmaking a station asks you to press twice. Changing a station's craft says the old licence is not refunded. A station you move stays a station.

## Shops
- Mages Guild and temple shelves (potions, magic items, soul gems) no longer restock when you close and reopen the window. They restock once a day.

## Android and controllers
- On short landscape phone screens (such as the AYN Thor), the main menu and its settings pages fit properly, and the sign-in window fits the screen with its Close button in reach.
- The intro, the main menu and its windows can be driven with a controller: the D-pad moves, A presses, B goes back (also from a text box).
- The four controller settings (look sensitivity, cursor sensitivity, movement threshold, deadzone) can now be changed. The "1.0" was a readout before. The stick deadzone stops at 90%.
- The three touch buttons in the bottom-right corner can be remapped in Settings, and Attack is one of the choices. A quick tap on Attack always swings.
