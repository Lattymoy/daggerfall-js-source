---- Custom quest to slay zombies and skeletons until a necromancer comes out of hiding.

Quest: CUSTOM01
DisplayName: From Dusk Till Dead

QRC:

QuestorOffer:  [1000]
<ce> Does the dark frighten you, %pcn? A
<ce> necromancer is raising zombies and
<ce> skeletons by the score at ___graveyard_.
<ce> We hold a contract to kill this villain
<ce> and end the evil. You will be paid
<ce> _reward_ gold. Will you take it?

RefuseQuest:  [1001]
<ce> A necromancer is no easy kill, %pcn.
<ce> I cannot blame you for saying no.

AcceptQuest:  [1002]
<ce> Good. Go to ___graveyard_ after nightfall. In
<ce> the graveyard, cut down its skeletons and
<ce> zombies until the necromancer is angry enough to
<ce> come out and face you. Return within =timeLimit_
<ce> days. Your _reward_ gold will be waiting.

QuestFail:  [1003]
<ce> You let me down, %pcn. Now the contract
<ce> goes to someone I can rely on.

QuestComplete:  [1004]
<ce> Well done, hero. Many innocent
<ce> people are alive because of you.

Message:  1020
<ce> The undead falls, and the ground shakes
<ce> under your feet. The necromancer has come!

Message:  1030
<ce> "You... will pay... with your
<ce> soul!" With that, the necromancer
<ce> falls dead. Without a master, the
<ce> undead will scatter and trouble
<ce> the villages no more.

Message:  1040
%qdt:
 The Fighters Guild of ___questgiver_ wants a
 necromancer dead at ___graveyard_. I must
 kill its skeletons and zombies there by
 night until it comes out. I have =timeLimit_
 days to finish and collect my pay.


QBN:

Item _reward_ gold

Person _questgiver_ group Questor male

Place _graveyard_ remote graveyard

Clock _timeLimit_ 00:00 0 flag 17 range 0 2

Foe _zombie_ is Zombie
Foe _skeleton_ is Skeletal_warrior
Foe _necromancer_ is Battle_mage

--	Quest start-up:
	start timer _timeLimit_
	reveal _graveyard_
	pc at _graveyard_ set _onsite_
	log 1040 step 0

_timeLimit_ task:
	end quest 

variable _onsite_
_daytime_ task:
	daily from 05:00 to 18:00

_attackPlayer_ task:
	when _onsite_ and not _daytime_ and not _necromancerDead_
	create foe _zombie_ every 5 minutes indefinitely with 60% success
	create foe _skeleton_ every 5 minutes indefinitely with 60% success

_summonNecromancer_ task:
	killed 4 _zombie_
	killed 4 _skeleton_
	say 1020
	create foe _necromancer_ every 1 minutes 1 times with 100% success

_necromancerDead_ task:
	killed _necromancer_
	say 1030

_questorClicked_ task:
	clicked npc _questgiver_

_clearclick_ task:
	when _questorClicked_ and not _necromancerDead_
	say 1003
	clear _questorClicked_ _clearclick_

_rewardPlayer_ task:
	when _questorClicked_ and _necromancerDead_
	give pc _reward_
	end quest
