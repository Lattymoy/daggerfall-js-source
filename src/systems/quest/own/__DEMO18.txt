---- Example quest to demonstrate RumoresDuringQuest and dialog link
---- get information after (and only after) hearing the correct rumor
---- once an npc talked about the correct rumor the linked dialog option
---- will be available in the tell me about section
---- only one of the two rumors will reveal the chat topic about the person
---- (you have to switch dialog option and then it will show up in the
---- tell me about section)
---- you can then ask peasants for the correct place where the person
---- can be found
---- one (and only one) of the three possible answers is revealing the correct place

Quest: __DEMO18

QRC:

QuestorOffer:  [1000]
<ce> People keep talking about a stranger in town.
<ce> Will you find out what the rumors are about?

RefuseQuest:  [1001]
<ce> A pity. I understand, but
<ce> I hoped you would say yes.

AcceptQuest:  [1002]
<ce> Good. Ask people what news and rumors they have heard.

RumorsDuringQuest:  [1005]
_thief_ is in town. You ought to meet %g2. Ask
around, and someone will know where _thief_ is.
<--->
Folk talk of a stranger in town,
but nobody can give me a name.


QuestLogEntry:  [1010]
%qdt:
 I am to ask people for news and
 rumors of a stranger nobody can name.

Message:  1011
They call _thief_ a master thief.
<--->
_thief_ runs the Thieves Guild's errands.
<--->
_thief_? That is the =thief_
who drinks at _tavern_.


QBN:

Person _thief_ face 81 faction The_Thieves_Guild anyInfo 1011
Place _tavern_ local tavern
