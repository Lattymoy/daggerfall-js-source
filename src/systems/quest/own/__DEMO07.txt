-- BAY-OWN: a demo of the Person resource that names Daggerfall's own King Gothryd and Queen Aubk-i.
---- Example quest used to test Person resource
---- Send player on a hunt to click both the King and Queen of Daggerfall

Quest: __DEMO07


QRC:

QuestLogEntry:  [1010]
%qdt
    Find _king_ at _dirtypit_, an
 inn in __dirtypit_. Then call on
 _queen_ at Castle Daggerfall.

Message: 1030
%pcn, your click found _king_.

Message: 1035
%pcn, your click found _queen_.

Message: 1040
Good work, %pcn. You have clicked both
the King and the Queen of Daggerfall.


QBN:

Place _dirtypit_ local tavern

Person _king_ named King_Gothryd
Person _queen_ named Queen_Aubk-i atHome


---- Quest startup
log 1010 step 0
create npc at _dirtypit_
place npc _king_ at _dirtypit_

_clickedKing_ task:
clicked npc _king_
say 1030

_clickedQueen_ task:
clicked npc _queen_
say 1035

_clickedBoth_ task:
when _clickedKing_ and _clickedQueen_
say 1040
remove log step 0
end quest
