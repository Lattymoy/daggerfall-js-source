Quest: __DEMO13


QRC:

Message:  1020
<ce> Five of them dead. Now to get out of here.

Message:  1030
%qdt:
 Someone drugged me and left me in a dungeon
 to die. Harpies circle overhead. I will not
 be their supper. I mean to kill five of
 them, escape, and find whoever did this.

Message: 1040
<ce> The room tilts as you drink from the cup. Too
<ce> late, you know it was drugged. You wake later in
<ce> a dark tomb, and something nearby is screeching.


QBN:

Place _mondung_ remote dungeon
Foe _monster_ is Harpy

--	Quest start-up:
	say 1040
	log 1030 step 0
	teleport pc to _mondung_
	pc at _mondung_ set _S.04_

_S.04_ task:
	create foe _monster_ every 8 minutes 10 times with 100% success 

_mondead_ task:
	killed 5 _monster_ saying 1020
	remove log step 0
