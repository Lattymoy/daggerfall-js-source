-- step 2 ends: the crafter's word is in the journal at once. The giver is unnamed (a court crafter): a Smith of the

-- words name no alchemy, and say a pot on the fire is supper, not a piece). Nothing is taken.
-- AUDIT QUESTLINES LINES-10: a Person is made fresh in each step's quest (a new name, in whatever town the player
-- stands in), so this step's giver is introduced as NEW - sent on by the last step's - never spoken of as the same one.
Quest: QLIP03
DisplayName: Made by Hand
-- Message panels
QRC:

QuestFail:  [1003]
<ce>         _crafter_ has stopped waiting for your piece.

QuestComplete:  [1004]
<ce>        "An apprentice's last wage. Spend it on tools."
<ce>          _crafter_ counts _gold_ gold into your hand.

QuestLogEntry:  [1010]
%qdt:
 Word again from the court crafter: a gatherer is
 only half a crafter. The other half is making. I
 am to make a piece of my own at a crafting
 station - a forge, a loom, a bench - and show it
 to _crafter_, who judges work for the court at
 _store_ in __store_.

Message:  1011
%qdt:
 I have made a piece of my own at a station.
 _crafter_ is waiting to hear of it at _store_
 in __store_.

Message:  1012
<ce>         "Gathering is half of it. Make something.
<ce>         The forge at an armorer's, the loom at a
<ce>         clothier's, the mason's bench here - any
<ce>         station will do. A pot on the fire is supper,
<ce>              not a piece. Then come back to me."

Message:  1013
<ce>        "You made it yourself? Start to finish?"
<ce>        _crafter_ asks after every step of the work,
<ce>          and nods at every answer.
<ce>
<ce>       "Then you need no master, and the court crafter
<ce>        has lost an apprentice. The court will hear your
<ce>        name one day. I will tell it you began with us."

Message:  1014
_crafter_ at _store_ judges work for the court. Waiting on an apprentice's piece, I hear.
<--->
_crafter_ makes for the court. Says the new apprentice will make a crafter yet.
<--->
Looking for _crafter_? Try _store_, at the mason's bench.

QBN:
Item _gold_ gold range 100 to 200

Person _crafter_ group Smith local anyInfo 1014
Place _store_ local generalstore

Clock _wait_ 30.00:00 0 flag 1 range 0 1

--	Quest start-up:
	place npc _crafter_ at _store_
	log 1010 step 0
	start timer _wait_

_made_ task:
	ql act piece-crafted 1
	log 1011 step 1

_clicked_ task:
	clicked npc _crafter_

_early_ task:
	when _clicked_ and not _made_
	say 1012
	clear _clicked_ _early_

_paid_ task:
	when _clicked_ and _made_
	say 1013
	give pc _gold_
	end quest

_wait_ task:
	say 1003
	end quest
