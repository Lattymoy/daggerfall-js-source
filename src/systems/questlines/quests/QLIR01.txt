-- the haulage. A wagon carries it if the player has one; the script cannot tell, and its words do not pretend to.

DisplayName: A Wagon’s Load
-- Message panels
QRC:

<ce>       _master_ has sent the load with someone else.

QuestComplete:  [1004]
<ce>          "The haulage, as agreed with _master_."
<ce>          _buyer_ counts _gold_ gold into your hand.

QuestLogEntry:  [1010]
%qdt:
 _master_, a stable master who does business at
 _inn_ in __inn_, sent me a note. A merchant
 pays well for a load hauled, and the stable master
 has one to send and no wagoner free to drive it. I
 am to collect it at _inn_.

Message:  1011
%pcn,

     A merchant at _yard_ in __yard_ has paid
 for a load of oats and harness, and every wagoner
 I know is out on the road. You look like someone
 who can sit a horse and mind a load.

     Come to _inn_ in __inn_ and I will hand it
 over. Haul it how you like - on a wagon if you
 have one, on your own back if you must. The
 merchant pays the haulage, and pays it well.

<ce>                           _master_, stable master

Message:  1012
<ce>          A stable lad smelling of hay and horse
<ce>          tugs your sleeve and hands you a note.
<ce>          "From _master_, at _inn_," he says.
<ce>               "There is a load waiting."

Message:  1013
<ce>        "Here it is - oats and harness, sewn up tight."
<ce>        _master_ heaves a heavy sack into your arms.
<ce>
<ce>       "_buyer_, at _yard_ in __yard_. And do not
<ce>        stop on the way to feed it to your own horse."

_master_ is always short of wagoners. The roads eat them.

Message:  1015
%qdt:
 _master_ gave me the load. I am to haul it to
 _buyer_ at _yard_ in __yard_, who pays the

<ce>
<ce>       "All there, and dry. _master_ chose well. If
<ce>        you ever haul for the yards again, come here."

Message:  1017
The merchant at _yard_? Buys oats and harness by the wagon, they say.
<--->
_buyer_ is waiting on a load from __inn_. Has been for days.
<--->
Looking for _buyer_? Try _yard_.

QBN:
Item _note_ letter used 1011
Item _load_ large_sack
Item _gold_ gold range 100 to 200

Person _master_ group Shopkeeper local anyInfo 1014
Person _buyer_ group Shopkeeper anyInfo 1017
Place _inn_ local tavern
Place _yard_ remote generalstore

Clock _courier_ 00:30 01:30
Clock _wait_ 30.00:00 0 flag 1 range 0 1

--	Quest start-up:
	place npc _master_ at _inn_
	place npc _buyer_ at _yard_
	start timer _courier_

_courier_ task:
	give pc _note_ notify 1012
	log 1010 step 0
	start timer _wait_

_loaded_ task:
	clicked npc _master_
	say 1013
	get item _load_

_told_ task:
	when _courier_ and _loaded_
	log 1015 step 1

_hauled_ task:
	toting _load_ and _buyer_ clicked
	say 1016
	give pc _gold_
	end quest

_wait_ task:
	say 1003
	end quest
