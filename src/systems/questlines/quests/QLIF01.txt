Quest: QLIF01
DisplayName: The First Star
-- Message panels
QRC:

QuestFail:  [1003]
<ce>        _seer_ has put your chart away, still blank.

QuestComplete:  [1004]
<ce>          "The stars pay those who read them. Here."
<ce>          _seer_ sets _gold_ gold upon your chart.

QuestLogEntry:  [1010]
%qdt:
 _seer_, an astrologer who reads the sky at
 _temple_ in __temple_, sent me a chart with my
 name on it. My fortune is written on the
 Firmament, the astrologer says, and I can write
 the rest myself. I am to take a star of my own,
 then go to _temple_ to have it read.

Message:  1011
%pcn,

     I cast your chart last night. I did not like
 it. Half of it is blank.

     That is no ill omen. It is an invitation. The
 sky is not fixed for such as you: every name you
 make for yourself in the realm earns you a star,
 and the stars you take become part of you.

     Take one. Any one. Then come to _temple_ in
 __temple_, and I will read what you chose.

<ce>                              _seer_, astrologer

Message:  1013
<ce>        _seer_ hears which star you took, and draws
<ce>          one bright point on your blank chart.
<ce>
<ce>       "There. The first star is the hardest to take.
<ce>        It tells the sky which way you mean to go."
<ce>        The astrologer taps the empty half of the
<ce>        chart. "The rest of it is waiting for you."

Message:  1014
The astrologer at _temple_? Reads the sky every night. Never sleeps.
<--->
_seer_ cast my chart once. Said I would marry well. I have not married at all.
<--->
Looking for _seer_? Try _temple_, on a clear night. Look up.

Message:  1015
%qdt:
 I have taken my first star on the Firmament.
 _seer_ is waiting to read it at _temple_ in
 __temple_.

Message:  1016
<ce>        "Your chart is still blank. Take a star -
<ce>         when your renown has earned you one - and
<ce>               then come back to me."

QBN:
Item _chart_ letter used 1011
Item _gold_ gold range 100 to 200

Person _seer_ group Spellcaster local anyInfo 1014
Place _temple_ local temple

Clock _wait_ 30.00:00 0 flag 1 range 0 1

--	Quest start-up:
	place npc _seer_ at _temple_
	start task _courier_

_courier_ task:
	get item _chart_
	log 1010 step 0
	start timer _wait_

_star_ task:
	ql act star-taken 1

_told_ task:
	when _courier_ and _star_
	log 1015 step 1

_clicked_ task:
	clicked npc _seer_

_early_ task:
	when _clicked_ and not _star_
	say 1016
	clear _clicked_ _early_

_paid_ task:
	when _clicked_ and _star_
	say 1013
	give pc _gold_
	end quest

_wait_ task:
	say 1003
	end quest
