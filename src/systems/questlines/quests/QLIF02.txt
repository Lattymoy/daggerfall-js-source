<ce>        the Serpent wanders where it likes and answers

<--->
Looking for _seer_? Try _temple_, on a clear night. Look up.

QBN:
Item _gold_ gold range 100 to 200

Person _seer_ group Spellcaster local anyInfo 1014
Place _temple_ local temple

Clock _wait_ 30.00:00 0 flag 1 range 0 1

--	Quest start-up:
	place npc _seer_ at _temple_
	log 1010 step 0
	start timer _wait_

_stars_ task:
	ql act star-taken 3
	log 1011 step 1

_clicked_ task:
	clicked npc _seer_

_early_ task:
	when _clicked_ and not _stars_
	say 1012
	clear _clicked_ _early_

_paid_ task:
	when _clicked_ and _stars_
	say 1013
	give pc _gold_
	end quest

_wait_ task:
	say 1003
	end quest
