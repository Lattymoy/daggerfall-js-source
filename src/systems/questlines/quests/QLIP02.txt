-- QL15 (2026-10-19; bible/06-Systems/Questlines-Arc.md section 4.5): THE COURT CRAFTER, step 2 of 3.
-- The port's own script on DFU's machine (systems/questlines/qlLines.js). Started by the line's runner an hour after
-- step 1 ends: the crafter's word is in the journal at once. The giver is unnamed (a court crafter): a Smith of the
-- player's own town, at its general store. The acts are the features' (qlActs.js): ore or stone mined (Mining and
-- Quarrying, a vein's ore or a boulder's stone), wood chopped (Logging, or Foraging's axe). Nothing is taken.
-- AUDIT QUESTLINES LINES-10: a Person is made fresh in each step's quest (a new name, in whatever town the player
-- stands in), so this step's giver is introduced as NEW - sent on by the last step's - never spoken of as the same one.
Quest: QLIP02
DisplayName: Ore and Timber
-- Message panels
QRC:

QuestFail:  [1003]
<ce>        _crafter_ has given the heavy work to someone else.

QuestComplete:  [1004]
<ce>         "Metal for the forge, timber for the bench."
<ce>          _crafter_ counts _gold_ gold into your hand.

QuestLogEntry:  [1010]
%qdt:
 Word from the court crafter: heavier work, and a
 colleague to judge it - _crafter_, who keeps the
 court's stock at _store_ in __store_. I am to
 mine ore or stone three times and chop wood three
 times, and then go to _store_ to say so.

Message:  1011
%qdt:
 I have mined three times and chopped wood three
 times. _crafter_ is waiting to hear of it at
 _store_ in __store_.

Message:  1012
<ce>         "Three times at the rock, three at the tree.
<ce>          Come back when your arms ache, not before."

Message:  1013
<ce>        _crafter_ looks at your hands - the blisters,
<ce>          the splinters - and seems well pleased.
<ce>
<ce>       "The court's crafter said you would come back
<ce>        with blisters. Fish and herbs feed the kitchen
<ce>        and the still. Ore and timber are the bones of
<ce>        everything else. You have fed both now. Next,
<ce>        you make something of it yourself."

Message:  1014
_crafter_ at _store_ keeps the court's stock. Sends apprentices up the hills for ore, I hear.
<--->
_crafter_ is judging the court crafter's new apprentice, they say. Is that you?
<--->
Looking for _crafter_? Try _store_. Mind the timber stacked by the door.

-- Symbols used in the QRC file:
--
--              %qdt occurs 2 times.
--         _crafter_ occurs 8 times.
--            _gold_ occurs 1 time.
--           _store_ occurs 5 times.
--          __store_ occurs 2 times.

QBN:
Item _gold_ gold range 100 to 200

Person _crafter_ group Smith local anyInfo 1014
Place _store_ local generalstore

Clock _wait_ 30.00:00 0 flag 1 range 0 1

--	Quest start-up:
	place npc _crafter_ at _store_
	log 1010 step 0
	start timer _wait_

_ore_ task:
	ql act ore-mined 3

_timber_ task:
	ql act wood-chopped 3

_ready_ task:
	when _ore_ and _timber_
	log 1011 step 1

_clicked_ task:
	clicked npc _crafter_

_early_ task:
	when _clicked_ and not _ready_
	say 1012
	clear _clicked_ _early_

_paid_ task:
	when _clicked_ and _ready_
	say 1013
	give pc _gold_
	end quest

_wait_ task:
	say 1003
	end quest
