-- The port's own script on DFU's machine (systems/questlines/qlLines.js). Started by the line's runner while the
-- Starfall runs (`needs: ['starfall']`), never offered by a questor: the letter is the offer. A Wyrd Sister at a tavern's
-- door hands it over - the Witches' Festival's vendors are Glenmoril's own (`ql act wyrd-met 1`, said as her window
-- opens: scenes/witchesHost.js) - and if the player holds a fallen star before meeting one, Morwen saw its thread burn
-- and her raven brings a note instead (AUDIT STAR8 T1: not a courier's clock - online REST8 cuts a delay to the short
-- wait, two real minutes of play, and a raven on a clock would beat every Sister). One or the other, never both.
-- Witnessed (net/questlineClaims.js `star`, the j1 receipt): it waits on a fallen star held (`ql act star-held 1`),
-- then on the player at the Glenmoril Coven (`permanent GlenmorilCoven`, the lycanthropy cure's own place - $CUREWER).
-- A coven is an open-air place in Daggerfall - one block of the wilds (WITCAA00.RMB), no building and no dungeon, so no
-- quest marker to stand a person on - and DFU's own quests meet one as this does, by the player's arrival (`pc at`,
-- S0000007's). Morwen meets the player at its fire. Lore: canon - the Witches' Festival on the 13th of Frostfall
-- (TEXT.RSC 8392), the music in the woods (Q0C4XY04.QRC: "The witches must be celebrating"), the Glenmoril Witches who
-- summon Hircine alone (FACTION.TXT 419, `summon: 4`), Mephala's summoning day the Festival's own
-- (systems/daedraSummoning.js). The port's own: the covens' rite that sings a star down and binds it in black thread,
-- Morwen of Glenmoril, her sisters at the taverns' doors, her raven.

QBN:
Item _letter_ letter used 1011
Item _note_ letter used 1017
Item _gold_ gold range 300 to 600

Place _glenmoril_ permanent GlenmorilCoven

Clock _wait_ 60.00:00 0 flag 1 range 0 1

--	Quest start-up:
	pc at _glenmoril_ set _atcoven_ saying 1018

variable _atcoven_

_wyrd_ task:
	ql act wyrd-met 1

_held_ task:
	ql act star-held 1
	log 1015 step 1

_sister_ task:
	when _wyrd_ and not _raven_
	get item _letter_ saying 1016
	reveal _glenmoril_
	log 1010 step 0
	start timer _wait_

_raven_ task:
	when _held_ and not _sister_
	get item _note_ saying 1012
	reveal _glenmoril_
	start timer _wait_

_met_ task:
	when _held_ and _atcoven_
	say 1013
	say 1014
	give pc _gold_
	end quest

_wait_ task:
	say 1003
	end quest
