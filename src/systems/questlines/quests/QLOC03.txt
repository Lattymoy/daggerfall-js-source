     The bells. Every harbor from Sentinel to
 Wayrest. The harbormasters call it a great serpent
 and ring its waters on the charts. We know its name.

     When the bells ring, sail out. Do not go alone if
 you can help it - it has sunk better ships than
 yours, and better than mine. When it is dead, find
 me at _inn_ in __inn_. There is one thing left to
 do, and the old sailors would want it done right.

<ce>                              Hamid al-Rasheen

Item _letter_ letter used 1011

Person _hamid_ faction People_of_Sentinel male anyInfo 1014

Clock _courier_ 01:00 03:00
Clock _wait_ 60.00:00 0 flag 1 range 0 1

--	Quest start-up:
	ql name _hamid_ Hamid_al-Rasheen
	place npc _hamid_ at _inn_
	start timer _courier_

_courier_ task:
	give pc _letter_ notify 1012
	log 1010 step 0
	start timer _wait_

_slain_ task:
	ql act serpent-slain 1
	log 1015 step 1

_qgclicked_ task:
	clicked npc _hamid_

_clearclick_ task:
	when _qgclicked_ and not _slain_
	say 1013
	clear _qgclicked_ _clearclick_

_home_ task:
	when _slain_ and _qgclicked_
	say 1020
	ask 1030 choices 1031 _toSea_ 1032 _keepScale_

variable _toSea_
variable _keepScale_

_poured_ task:
	when _toSea_
	say 1033
	outcome _offering_
	give pc _gold_
	end quest

_kept_ task:
	when _keepScale_
	say 1034
	outcome _scale_
	give pc _gold_
	end quest

_wait_ task:
	say 1003
	end quest
