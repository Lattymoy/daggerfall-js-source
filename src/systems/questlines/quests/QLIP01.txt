-- The port's own script on DFU's machine (systems/questlines/qlLines.js). Started by the line's runner, never offered
-- by a questor: the note is the offer. An introduction: the Tutorials explain, this gives a reason to go and pays for
-- it. The giver is unnamed (a court crafter): a Smith of the player's own town, at its general store, where the
-- Mason's bench stands (tutorialCatalogue.js, crafting). The acts are the features' (qlActs.js): a fish caught, a
-- plant gathered - Professions or Foraging. Nothing is taken: the crafter hears what was found.
-- AUDIT QUESTLINES LINES-26: the letter signs off on one line, its sender's name and calling - DFU's pack names a
-- quest letter by its last line (ItemHelper.cs:335-348, itemInfo.js questLetterName).
Quest: QLIP01
DisplayName: Of Rod and Root
-- Message panels
QRC:

QuestFail:  [1003]
<ce>          _crafter_ has found another apprentice.

QuestComplete:  [1004]
<ce>         "Fair pay for honest work. Buy a better rod."
<ce>          _crafter_ counts _gold_ gold into your hand.

QuestLogEntry:  [1010]
%qdt:
 _crafter_, who makes for the court, sent me a
 note. The crafter wants an apprentice who finds
 their own materials, and will pay to see that I
 can: three fish caught and three plants gathered,
 then back to _store_ in __store_ to say so.

Message:  1011
%pcn,

     The court keeps my hands full, and I need
 another pair. Not a pair that waits for the
 carter - a pair that goes out and finds the stock.

     Catch three fish. Gather three plants, any
 that grow wild and are worth the picking. Then
 come to _store_ in __store_ and we will
 talk about wages.

<ce>                       _crafter_, crafter to the court

QBN:
Item _note_ letter used 1011
Item _gold_ gold range 100 to 200

Person _crafter_ group Smith local anyInfo 1014
Place _store_ local generalstore

Clock _courier_ 00:30 01:30
Clock _wait_ 30.00:00 0 flag 1 range 0 1

--	Quest start-up:
	place npc _crafter_ at _store_
	start timer _courier_

_courier_ task:
	give pc _note_ notify 1012
	log 1010 step 0
	start timer _wait_

_fish_ task:
	ql act fish-caught 3

_herbs_ task:
	ql act herb-gathered 3

_ready_ task:
	when _fish_ and _herbs_

_told_ task:
	when _courier_ and _ready_
	log 1015 step 1

_clicked_ task:
	clicked npc _crafter_

_early_ task:
	when _clicked_ and not _ready_
	say 1016
	clear _clicked_ _early_

_paid_ task:
	when _clicked_ and _ready_
	say 1013
	give pc _gold_
	end quest

_wait_ task:
	say 1003
	end quest
