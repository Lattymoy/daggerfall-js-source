-- harbourmaster's note is the offer and the word, the harbourmaster waiting where the player stood when it was taken
-- (`local tavern`). The act: a port reached under sail (`ql act port-reached 1` - systems/questlines/qlActs.js, the
-- harbour office's berth); then back to the harbourmaster for the purse. The giver is unnamed, as the registry's
-- (qlLines.js "a harbourmaster").

QuestLogEntry:  [1010]
%qdt:
 A note came from _master_, a harbormaster who
 drinks at _tavern_ here in __tavern_ and needs a
 captain. I am to take a ship out of any port and
 bring her into the harbor of another, a quarter
 league off or more, then come back to _tavern_ for
 my purse.

Message:  1011
To whoever reads this with a ship in mind,

     I have more cargo than captains, and more
 captains in the taverns than at sea. Prove you are
 not one of them. Take a ship - your own, or one
 bought at a shipyard - out of any port, and bring
 her into the harbor of another a quarter league
 off or more. Not by the road. Sail.

     Then come and find me at _tavern_, and
 there is a purse in it.

<ce>                             _master_, harbormaster

Message:  1013
%qdt:
 I have brought a ship into port under sail.
 _master_ waits at _tavern_ in __tavern_ to hear
 of it.

Message:  1014
<ce>         "Sailed yet? No? The sea is not in this tavern,
<ce>          friend. Out of one port and into the harbor of
<ce>          another, a quarter league off at the least, and
<ce>          then we talk."

Message:  1015
<ce>         "Into port under your own sail! The coast needs
<ce>          captains, and now it has one more. Come back
<ce>          when you are ready for real work."

Message:  1016
The harbormaster drinks at _tavern_. Says nobody goes to sea any more.
<--->
_master_? Runs the harbor. Looking for a captain, if you can call yourself one.
<--->
There's a harbormaster at _tavern_ handing out work to anyone with a ship.

QBN:
Item _note_ letter used 1011
Item _gold_ gold range 200 to 400

Person _master_ group Resident1 anyInfo 1016
Place _tavern_ local tavern

Clock _wait_ 30.00:00 0 flag 1 range 0 1

--	Quest start-up:
	place npc _master_ at _tavern_
	start task _word_

_word_ task:
	get item _note_
	log 1010 step 0
	start timer _wait_

_sailed_ task:
	ql act port-reached 1
	log 1013 step 1

_qgclicked_ task:
	clicked npc _master_

_clearclick_ task:
	when _qgclicked_ and not _sailed_
	say 1014
	clear _qgclicked_ _clearclick_

_paid_ task:
	when _sailed_ and _qgclicked_
	say 1015
	give pc _gold_
	end quest

_wait_ task:
	say 1003
	end quest
