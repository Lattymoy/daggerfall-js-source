-- black thread at their coven nearest the capital (`permanent BluffCoven`, the region of Daggerfall). A coven is an
-- open-air place with no quest marker (WITCAA04.RMB - QLTF01 says why), so its people come out of the dark when the
-- player arrives, as DFU's own ambushes do (`create foe ... 1 times`, started by the arrival, so a return while the
-- coven stands brings no second): the Spinner and three of her sisters, each named and stood as a woman (`ql name ...
-- female`, systems/questlines/qlActions.js QlName - Foe.cs SetFoeName rolls a man as often as not). AUDIT STAR8 S1: the
-- spindle is the player's as the Spinner falls (`get item`, never on her body - a body the cull, a teleport or a load
-- takes would take it too), and a coven gone unfought (`ql gone`: the Spinner unkilled and none of them standing - the
-- encounters' 120 m cull, a death's teleport) is called up again at the next arrival; her death costs the Sisters of
-- the Bluff's favour (`change repute with`, a stand-in of their faction - A0C0XY04's `_dummymage_` way). Then Morwen at
-- the Glenmoril Coven's fire, met once on the player's arrival with the spindle, and her question: a length unwound to
-- sell (`unwound`, and Morwen buys it herself rather than see it sold) or the spindle given whole (`wound`); with the
-- spindle in hand her patience has no end (`stop timer`). Lore: canon - the Sisters of the Bluff (FACTION.TXT 423), the
-- Coven on the Bluff (MAPS.BSA, the quest table's BluffCoven). The port's own: the Spinner, her spindle, the starlight
-- spun on it.

QBN:
Item _letter_ letter used 1011
Item _spindle_ trinket used 1017
Item _gold_ gold range 300 to 600

Place _glenmoril_ permanent GlenmorilCoven
Place _bluff_ permanent BluffCoven

Clock _courier_ 01:00 03:00
Clock _wait_ 60.00:00 0 flag 1 range 0 1

Person _bluffsister_ face 1 faction The_Sisters_of_the_Bluff remote

Foe _spinner_ is Sorcerer
Foe _sisters_ is 3 Nightblade

--	Quest start-up:
	ql name _spinner_ The_Spinner female
	ql name _sisters_ Sister_of_the_Bluff female
	pc at _bluff_ set _atbluff_ saying 1015
	pc at _glenmoril_ set _atcoven_
	have _spindle_ set _hasspindle_
	start timer _courier_

variable _atbluff_
variable _atcoven_

_courier_ task:
	give pc _letter_ notify 1012
	reveal _bluff_
	reveal _glenmoril_
	log 1010 step 0
	start timer _wait_

_arrived_ task:
	when _courier_ and _atbluff_
	start task _ambush_

_lost_ task:
	ql gone _spinner_ _sisters_
	clear _arrived_ _ambush_ _lost_

_ambush_ task:
	create foe _spinner_ every 0 minutes 1 times with 100% success msg 1019
	create foe _sisters_ every 0 minutes 1 times with 100% success

_spinnerslain_ task:
	killed 1 _spinner_ saying 1013
	get item _spindle_
	change repute with _bluffsister_ by -10

_sistersslain_ task:
	killed 3 _sisters_

_hasspindle_ task:
	stop timer _wait_
	say 1014
	log 1016 step 1

_back_ task:
	when _hasspindle_ and _atcoven_
	start task _home_

_home_ task:
	say 1020
	ask 1030 choices 1031 _unwind_ 1032 _keepwhole_

variable _unwind_
variable _keepwhole_

_soldway_ task:
	when _unwind_
	say 1033
	outcome _unwound_
	give pc _gold_
	end quest

_wholeway_ task:
	when _keepwhole_
	say 1034
	outcome _wound_
	give pc _gold_
	end quest

_wait_ task:
	say 1003
	end quest
