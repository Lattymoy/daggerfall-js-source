# Patch Notes: Sealed dungeons, house fixes, gold and party travel

## Loading
- Morrowind models: your character's model starts building as the game loads, alongside the world, not after it. Its textures load in the background, so the game hitches less while it builds.
- Continue and the start menu find your latest save faster.

## Dungeons
- The gaps in the original game's stairs and curved ceilings are sealed. No more seeing into the void, or into other rooms, through them.

## Homes
- The decorating camera stays inside the room: no more flying through walls, floors or ceilings, so nothing gets placed where you can't see it.
- A piece aimed at the ceiling hangs from it instead of vanishing above it. The first Lower press moves it down.
- Online: a piece you just placed no longer disappears when the room finishes loading.
- A piece whose model fails to load is tried again instead of staying invisible.
- The camera no longer gets stuck inside a big piece you just placed around it.
- Item pictures show their material everywhere: pack, hotbar, HUD and wall. Daedric looks Daedric, not steel.
- Mod weapons show their own picture on the wall the first time.
- With Morrowind models on, hung weapons and armour show as their Morrowind models.

## Gold
- You can drop gold again, and store it in your own storage: the ship's chest, house cupboards and placed chests. The Gold button is on your pack's footer: Drop gold, Store gold or Stow gold.

## Party (online)
- Party travel is on the Party tab.
  - The leader: Travel together (or pick a place on the travel map), and Call off.
  - Members: Ready, Stay behind, or Travel to the leader.
  - The chat commands still work.

## Fast travel
- A and D strafe again during a Travel Options journey, so you can step around obstacles. The journey carries on from where you stepped.
- Journeys get stuck going back and forth at obstacles far less often.
