# Patch Notes: Climates & Calories difficulty, and fixes

## New: Climates & Calories now has Off / Casual / Hard
- Pick it in Features. **Casual is the new default.**
- **Casual:** the same world with gentler costs. A need only costs stamina, only while it shows red on your HUD, and never takes you below half your stamina bar. You get that stamina back once you eat, drink, sleep or warm up. No health or attribute loss, no food sickness, nothing refused. A lit campfire keeps the cold off.
- **Hard:** the full mod as before. Needs can hurt you and drain attributes, bad food can make you sick, and sleeping rough leaves you stiff.
- **Off:** the classic game, with no needs and no survival items. Your camps are kept, not deleted. You can see other players' campfires but can't use them.
- New Casual and Hard characters start with rations, a waterskin, camping gear and a fire kit. Off characters start with the classic bag.
- Switching settings keeps your progress. Time spent on Off doesn't count against your needs.

## Online
- Everyone picks their own setting.
- Corpses now always carry food (meat, rations), whoever's game handled the kill.

## Fixes
- Hitching your horse no longer shows two wagons.
- The "Wagon" label no longer flickers while your wagon trails behind you.
- When an NPC says they'll mark someone's house on your map, it now shows up on the town map.
- Camps no longer follow you on fast travel or duplicate when you load a save.
- Your camps stay yours after a refresh or in a new tab.
- "Rest here" at a camp needs you close to the fire, and resting at your own tent keeps its fire going.
- Need messages no longer repeat while a need is getting better, and fast travel no longer floods the screen with them.
- Other players' campfires far away no longer switch off nearby street lamps.
- Plus smaller fixes to survival messages, water, food and sleep.
